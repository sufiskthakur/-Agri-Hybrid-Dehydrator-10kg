# Open-Source Hybrid 12V 10 kg Agricultural Dehydrator

A low-cost, off-grid compatible food dehydrator optimized for rural smallholders and local village fabrication. Designed to operate via free solar-air thermal collection, a 12V battery backup (tractor/inverter batteries), or standard grid power.

## Key Features
- **Capacity:** 10 kg fresh produce across 10 removable stainless steel trays.
- **Power Flexibility:** Runs on 12V DC (Solar / Spare Farm Batteries) or 230V AC Grid (via standard 12V adapter). Zero need for expensive inverters.
- **Thermal Hybrid Design:** Uses a passive solar-air pre-heating collector combined with a low-wattage 12V silicone heating pad controlled by an STC-1000 thermostat.
- **Compact Footprint:** 60 × 60 × 90 cm structure that fits easily into small farm sheds or household spaces.

## Bill of Materials & Cost Estimate
Estimated local build cost: **₹10,500 – ₹15,300** (excluding solar panel and spare battery already owned by the user). See `electrical-schematics/bill-of-materials.csv` for details.
